About

A small studio with a big checklist.

Amberwick is a small independent studio making stylized props for Unity developers. We started by building assets for our own projects and got tired of the same problems in every pack we bought: wrong scale, messy hierarchies, materials that fell apart in a different pipeline.

Rustic Farm Kit is our first release and, honestly, a test of the waters. Everything in it is checked in a real Unity 6 project before it goes on the store. If it works out, more packs follow, shaped by what people actually ask for.

FAQ
Which Unity versions are supported?

Packs are built and tested in Unity 6. Supported versions are listed on each store page.

Which render pipelines are supported?

URP and HDRP. Textures include a Mask Map for HDRP and a Metallic-Smoothness map for URP. The Built-in render pipeline is not supported.

Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

Yes. All assets are sold under the standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use in commercial projects. Reselling the source assets themselves is not allowed.

What texture sizes and formats are included?

Each prop has its own PBR texture set, 1024×1024 for most props and 2048×2048 for larger pieces. Models come as FBX and GLB.

What about refunds?

Refunds are handled by Unity according to the Asset Store refund policy. If something is broken on our side, write to us first, we'll fix it fast.
